# BuildFlow — Initial Zimbabwe Contractor Discovery Prospect List

**Prepared:** 24 November 2026  
**Status:** Desk-researched lead hypotheses only; none contacted, qualified, endorsed or confirmed as a prospective customer.  
**Purpose:** Build an initial interview pipeline for validating the BuildFlow MVP with construction businesses. This is not a marketing mailing list.

## 1. Qualification principle

Prioritise contractors likely to experience multi-project coordination, BOQ-to-procurement handoffs, site-to-office reporting and client update challenges. A public directory listing does not establish company size, software needs, budget, current project activity or interest in BuildFlow. Confirm all of these in a discovery conversation.

The first cohort should favour SMEs with an active, manageable project and an owner/operations lead willing to sponsor a pilot. Larger contractors can be useful for benchmarking but may require deeper ERP, procurement, compliance or integration capabilities outside the MVP.

## 5. Outreach sequence (manual approval required)

Do not send bulk messages. Suggested sequence:
1. Select 8–12 candidates across small building contractors, civils contractors and design-build firms.
2. Verify each organisation's official contact route and identify an owner, director, operations or project-management contact.
3. Send a short, individually addressed request for a 20–30 minute research interview; do not pitch an unapproved product or claim the product is ready.
4. Explain that the discussion is product research, not a sales commitment.
5. Ask permission before recording or retaining personal contact details.
6. Capture workflow insights and willingness-to-pay evidence in a controlled discovery log.
7. Invite only qualified participants to a later pilot discussion after the technical and legal release gates pass.

## 6. Interview objectives

For each interview, establish:
- Active projects and typical project size/complexity.
- Roles involved in project setup, BOQ, procurement, site reporting and client updates.
- Current tools and where data is duplicated or lost.
- Typical delay between purchase request and decision.
- How changes/variations affect BOQ, budgets and approvals.
- How site updates reach the office and client.
- Which information is too sensitive to place in a cloud application without safeguards.
- Current software spend and total cost of implementation/training/support.
- Who approves software purchases and the evidence they require.
- Interest in a structured pilot, subject to readiness and terms.
